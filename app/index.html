Roadmap to the platinum — Vice Got a Price!

PowerPyx rates the platinum 4/10 difficulty and about 200 hours, one playthrough, with free roam after the story and no mission replay. There are no online trophies and no difficulty trophies. Do not use cheats on your trophy save — PowerPyx reports cheats disable trophies and saving.

    100% completion, in short

    The checklist (Progress > Stats > 100% Completion, visible only after the story) is about 60% true completion: 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals, 70 of 80 challenges, 15 of 18 card sets and meditations, 300 of 397 stunts. Only robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret’s quests need everything. Nothing on it is missable. Source: PowerPyx 100% checklist.

    There are no online trophies in GTA VI — the whole list is single-player and offline. (Sources: PowerPyx; Push Square.)
